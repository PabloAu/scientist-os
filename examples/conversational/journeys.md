# Conversational acceptance prompts

These are simulated scientist requests for a fictional fixture, not a transcript,
not canned model responses, and not human scientific approval. A capable host
must actually select and operate tools, inspect outputs and record its answers.

1. Start an empty project: "I am exploring whether a correction reduces residual
   drift in a microscopy phantom. Help me frame what we can learn and what you
   need to know. Save our context. Everything supplied here is fictional."
2. "Incorporate the selected mixed folder. Inspect the original paper figure and
   slide, explain source authority, and retain anything excluded or unresolved."
3. "Register the planned experiment, protocol and material. Discuss competing
   explanations and controls. The actual execution note and data arrived later;
   reconcile them without rewriting the original plan."
4. "Run the supplied versioned Python module on the explicit CSV. Use the fixture's
   declared independent units. Record all inputs and assumptions, make a figure
   and replay it. Explain what the comparison does and does not establish."
5. "Use the fictional study sheets to conduct a controlled meta-analysis. Record
   eligibility, exclusions, exact extraction, independence, comparability and
   sensitivity. Do not treat a teaching freeze as human scientific approval."
6. "Make an editable lab meeting deck, a short proposal and a bounded Results and
   Methods document with figure and supplement. Show the artifacts. Revise a
   selected passage to make the uncertainty clearer, preserving its history."
7. "The supplied correction changes the measurement units or an observation.
   Incorporate the corrected source, identify affected claims and files, and
   recompute or revise while retaining historical runs."
8. "Pause after recording the next step. In a fresh host context, continue using
   only the installed skill and durable project state. Do not repeat completed
   actions. An external action with no completion receipt must remain uncertain."
9. "For a mock reviewer round, freeze the exact draft and address a request about
   pseudoreplication and a request for a new experiment. Record the smallest
   adequate response and any scientific decision needed. Capture one actual
   encountered workflow failure, verify its fix and name its limits."

Assess completion, support/routing, numerical correctness, input/code/output
provenance, useful abstention, corrections, recovery and required human effort.
Report engineering checks, real host inference and human validation separately.
