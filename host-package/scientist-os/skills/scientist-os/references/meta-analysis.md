# Controlled meta-analysis

Meta-analysis is a governed research workflow across stages 0, 4, 5 and 6. A pooling function alone does not establish a systematic review, valid comparability or absence of bias. Keep planning, selection, extraction, scientific approval, synthesis and interpretation distinct.

## M01 — Question, eligibility and search

Define population/model, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, estimand, study design, context and intended inference. Specify eligibility/exclusion rules, search sources/queries/date limits, language/publication/access handling and planned synthesis/sensitivity before screening outcomes. Record later amendments and why; do not portray retrospective decisions as prespecified.

Register every considered study with identifiers/version, provenance and duplicate-family links. Deduplicate publications and overlapping samples/cohorts; a different paper ID is not proof of independence. Preserve search dates/results and the disposition of inaccessible material. Titles/abstracts guide screening; relied-upon extraction requires original full-text/tables/figures. Record included, excluded with reasons, awaiting information and unresolved candidates, along with the actual selection counts. Do not invent a comprehensive search or dual independent review.

## M02 — Traceable extraction and comparability

For each candidate effect retain original locator/quote or table cell/figure region, original estimate/uncertainty/sample size/units, definition/timepoint, transformations and derivation, extractor/checker and actual inspection evidence. Distinguish reported values, digitized values and calculated estimates; record digitization uncertainty. Reconcile original numbers and labels visually when a table/figure is involved. Use exact source revisions and keep excluded extractions.

Assess estimand/direction/scale/unit compatibility, interventions/conditions/timepoints, measurement methods, population/model differences, design limitations, risk of bias, and dependency structure before pooling. Record multiple outcomes/effects per study, shared controls, duplicate cohorts and why selected effects are independent or modeled appropriately. Unknown comparability or dependence is an unresolved gate, not a default assumption.

Lead review applies to material inclusion, outcome/estimand choice, transformations, comparability decisions and synthesis plan. Already specified plans authorize routine extraction/calculation; do not ask for repeated operational permission. Freeze the eligible extraction set, exact inputs and analysis choices before synthesis. Changing selection creates a versioned amendment and invalidates the dependent synthesis.

## M03 — Synthesis, sensitivity and reporting

Choose a method that fits the estimand/design and dependence. The supplied inverse-variance fixed and DerSimonian–Laird random-effects routine has normal-interval and small-study limitations; use a different versioned Python module when required and available. Do not pool incompatible or known overlapping estimates through that calculator. A heterogeneity statistic is not proof that studies are comparable or unbiased.

Record weights, pooled estimate/interval, heterogeneity, method assumptions and all contributions. Prespecify appropriate leave-one-out, alternative model/estimator, study-quality, dependence and eligibility sensitivity. Do not silently omit a failed sensitivity or choose the most favorable result. Assess robustness of the substantive conclusion, not only statistical significance. Small study counts limit heterogeneity and publication-bias assessments; absence of a detectable signal is not absence of bias.

Execute and replay with the full [analysis run contract](analysis-panels.md). Produce an editable extraction/selection table, synthesis/forest plot and evidence-bounded text with exclusions, uncertainty, caveats and search limits. Link figure rows to exact extraction/source/run identities. If pooling is unjustified, deliver structured qualitative comparison and the missing-information decision instead of a manufactured summary estimate.

## M04 — Failure and re-entry

Missing originals, incompatible units, unresolvable cohorts, invalid uncertainty, extraction conflicts or changed eligibility block dependent pooling. Continue source acquisition/reconciliation or descriptive tables. New studies/corrections re-open eligibility and comparability, mark synthesis and related claims/artifacts stale, and retain the earlier frozen set and run. Sensitivity results that undermine the conclusion must change the interpretation honestly.
