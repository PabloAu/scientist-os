# Evidence-bounded writing and editable artifacts

## W01 — Stage 6: draft from a claim map

Start from the scientist's purpose/audience, current narrative spine, exact evidence/panel state, claim-evidence map, terminology and artifact selection. Retrieve and verify support before writing. Draft one coherent section/panel/slide or a scoped set as requested; conversation can refine tone, focus, structure and scientific meaning. Do not force a series of forms or require manually selecting every supporting record.

For claims requiring unresolved evidence, use a precise placeholder naming the missing observation/analysis/source/decision and its owner. Do not write confident substitute prose or attach an adjacent citation to unsupported wording. Distinguish measured observations, model interpretation, hypotheses, proposed experiments and validation. Quantify only from current governed results, carrying units, independent n, exclusions and uncertainty.

Alongside proposed scientific prose retain four concise reports: claim-to-evidence/state and permitted limits; citation accuracy/fit/original checks; terminology/operational meaning and conflicts; changes with rationale, affected artifacts and unresolved questions. Routine minor wording edits can have a brief report proportional to their scope. A rewrite that changes estimand, causal meaning, exclusions or claim strength needs a scientific decision, not merely a style approval.

## W02 — Coupled outputs

Create professional editable manuscripts/proposals, presentation slides, figures/captions and supplements using the host's appropriate tools. Keep the scientist-facing artifact free of internal implementation chatter unless relevant to reproducibility. Keep editable source plus final exports and link each section/panel/slide to its evidence and run. A proposal describes proposed work and feasibility, never presents planned results as observed.

Reconcile facts across Abstract, Results, Methods, captions, tables, references, slides and supplements: sample/independent counts, exclusions, units, parameters, software revisions, statistical methods, terminology, labels and claim scope. Methods describes what actually ran and actual protocol applicability; a intended/default pipeline is not historical execution. Tables/equations/multi-panel images need tool-specific checks; declare unsupported editing/rendering instead of claiming full fidelity.

## W03 — Exact artifact selection and revision

Resolve the scientist's selected passage, panel or slide to an exact artifact identity/revision and locator. Preserve the before version and a reviewable change. Validate that the selection/current revision still matches before application; stale targets require reconciliation. Use named actual actor and reason; an assistant applying an authorized draft edit remains an assistant-authored unreviewed revision, never impersonates human approval. Keep evidence links and change report through export and reimport.

Use native host preview/annotation when available or a focused scientific view. If the host cannot expose a selection directly, use exact quoted passage plus section/page/slide and revision, or open the artifact for a supported selection path. User edits to the file create a new artifact identity and require evidence/fact reconciliation before overwriting anything.

## W04 — Render, inspect and promote

Render and inspect the actual generated artifact, every relevant page/slide and edited region at useful resolution. Check clipping, overflow, hierarchy, legibility, tables/equations, labels/units, legend/color accessibility, figure sharpness, editable content, numbering/cross-references and numerical/scientific consistency. Record exact artifact hash, renderer/version, pages/slides inspected, findings and fixes. A successful export or a prior template's QA does not verify the current document. If rendering is unavailable, provide the editable file and explicit unverified layout status; do not certify it.

Professional appearance does not promote evidence. The lead reviews scientific content and any required coauthor/governance roles approve their scope. Drafts may be useful while gaps are explicit. Submission-ready text cannot contain unresolved factual placeholders or depend on stale evidence; reopen the owning inventory/corpus/analysis procedure first.

## W05 — Stage 7: integrated submission gate

Review scientific reasoning, statistics, measurement semantics, complete provenance, citations/terminology, figures/supplements, accessibility, confidentiality/rights and governance roles. Check current official venue requirements when actually targeting a journal: article type, limits, reporting/data/code statements, forms and required files. A journal checklist from an earlier date is not current evidence.

Reconcile every claimed result and cross-document fact, remaining problems, panel gates and exact artifact approvals. Prepare and inspect exact release/submission content, with manifest of filenames, versions/hashes, locations, date and approving roles. Only perform external submission/sharing within explicit applicable authorization; creating the package is routine work within the task. Freeze the actual submitted package and acknowledgement when submission occurs. Never reconstruct it later from memory.

Failures or new evidence reopen the narrowest owning stage. A changed figure may alter caption, Results and summary slides; preserve the previous approved/submitted snapshot rather than replacing history.
